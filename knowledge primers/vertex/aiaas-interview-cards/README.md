# Vertex AIaaS Interview Cards

Interview cards for the Vertex Inc **Senior Principal Engineer, AIaaS Enablement (Commercial AI Center of Excellence)** role, built on 21 October 2026 from the job description. Each line of the job description gets one card. Each card has six tabs: what the line means, your evidence for it, how you would do it on Azure, where it goes wrong, the code, and the one-minute answer. Three reference cards close the page: the script, a map of the job description against your evidence, and the probes to expect. The page is a companion to the long-form article in `../aiaas-on-azure/`. It reuses that article's Azure specifics and adds what this job description asks for and the article did not cover: hands-on fine-tuning, training-data governance, tool-surface design, retrieval internals, memory, and growing Principal and Staff engineers.

## The scenario cards

Added on 22 October 2026 for the scenario-based round. Every scenario A–I and L has the same pattern figure at the top of its Agentic logic tab, generated by `patdiag.py` from a short spec: the steps as boxes coloured by who decides (code, model, model in batch, person, write), the tools under each step and where they live (MCP server, in-process, code, gateway alias, control plane, wrapped vendor), and a dashed box on the deterministic check that stands between the model and the side effect. Card J holds the rules the figures follow; card K holds the questions each scenario opens with. Scenario numbers are illustrative and reuse the page's shared set (900 + 60 tokens per product, $2.85 per 1,000 on the frontier alias, 15% escalated, 0.94 → 0.96).

## The glossary

Generated from plain-text source files (one per topic) by a small build script that checks every cross-reference. The topics are platform & architecture, Azure & cloud services, models & training, data & governance, agents & tools, retrieval & context, evaluation & observability, safety & responsible AI, cost & reliability, leadership, metrics & statistics, Vertex & tax, and your projects. Look-alike terms are spelled out where they cause confusion. For example: LLM tokens vs privacy tokens vs access tokens, model quantisation vs vector quantisation, Google's Vertex AI vs Vertex Inc, and a cloud landing zone vs a data landing zone. Examples with numbers are illustrative unless they quote the resume or a dated source.

## Conventions

- **Chips:** `resume` = quoted verbatim from the resume sent to Vertex (all 72 quotes checked against it). `your account` = what you have said about the work, as recorded in the AIaaS article. `illustrative` = numbers and designs made up for an example. Dated tags = public sources.
- **Vertex products as settings:** worked examples use Smart Categorization, Vertex Copilot and the Configuration Agent for Dynamics 365. They are used only as settings for the examples, never as claims about how Vertex builds them.
- **Consistent illustrative numbers:** cards 2, 4 and 12 share one set of numbers. 900 input and 60 output tokens per product; $2.50 / $10 per million tokens for the frontier alias; $0.10 per 1,000 products for the fine-tuned model; 15% of products escalated. That gives 0.96 accuracy for the cascade and $2,850 → $314 per million products.
- **Palette:** asphalt neutrals with a lane-amber accent (light `#9a5800`, dark `#f2b347`). Group colours: direction `#1d4ed8`, models and data `#0f766e`, agents and retrieval `#6d28d9`, operations `#be185d`, people `#4d7c0f`, scenarios `#0e7490` (dark `#67e8f9`), references `#6b7280`. Charts use a single series colour (`#2f6fd1` / `#5d8fd6`), with the reference bar drawn as an outline.
- **Slugs** (append to the artifact URL as `#slug`): `vision`, `build-vs-buy`, `training-strategy`, `fine-tuning`, `training-data`, `orchestration`, `mcp-tools`, `retrieval`, `context-memory`, `evaluation`, `safety`, `cost-reliability`, `leadership`, `multiplying`, `scenarios`, `scenario-a-skus`, `scenario-b-config-agent`, `scenario-c-copilot`, `scenario-d-quarter-end`, `scenario-e-einvoicing`, `scenario-f-one-agent-or-many`, `scenario-g-fine-tune`, `scenario-h-security`, `scenario-i-three-rag-stacks`, `rules-patterns-planes-tools`, `requirements-questions`, `scenario-l-conversational-ai`, `script`, `jd-map`, `probes`, `glossary`. Glossary entries are `#g-<term-id>` and letters are `#gl-A` to `#gl-Z`.
